About
I help creators build videos their audience can't stop watching.

I'm Dalezio — a video editor based in Athens, Greece, working remotely with YouTube creators worldwide. My editing is built on a single principle: every second of a video should earn the next.

I specialize in IRL & Challenge content, Travel, Vlogs, and Fitness — the kind of videos where storytelling and pacing are everything.

My process starts with story structure. I treat every video like a short film — with a hook, rising tension, and a payoff — because that's what turns casual viewers into subscribers.

Capabilities

Everything your video needs.

A complete editing toolkit built for high-retention YouTube content.

Storytelling & Narrative
Structure raw footage into a story arc with a strong hook, rising tension, and clear payoff that keeps viewers watching.
Retention Editing
Precise cut timing, pattern interrupts, and pacing choices designed to prevent drop-off and maximize audience retention.
Sound Design
Multi-layer audio mixing, custom SFX, ambience, and music sync that makes editing feel immersive and professional.
Cinematic Color Grading
Colour correction and LUT-based grading for a consistent, premium look across all footage types and lighting conditions.
Pacing & Rhythm
Beat-matched cuts, dynamic editing rhythm, and controlled pacing that matches the energy of the content.
Motion Graphics
Clean, minimal on-screen graphics, lower thirds, and animated text elements that enhance without distracting.
Motion Tracking
Smooth tracked text, graphics, and effects attached to subjects or surfaces in the footage.
Music Sync
Precise editing to musical beats and emotional peaks, creating a satisfying, almost involuntary viewing experience.
